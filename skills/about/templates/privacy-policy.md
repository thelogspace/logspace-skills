## 1. Data we collect

The app does not ask for your name, address or phone number. The data below is collected **automatically** by the services the app uses:

<!-- IF firebase -->
### Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC)
- **Crash data:** device model, OS version, app version, crash stack trace.
- **Usage data:** screens viewed, in-app events, session duration, approximate location (country/city level), app instance ID.
- **Purpose:** to find errors and improve the app.
- Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
<!-- END firebase -->

<!-- IF admob -->
### Google AdMob (Google LLC)
- **Data:** advertising ID (IDFA/GAID, with your permission), IP address, device information, ad interactions.
- **Purpose:** to show ads and measure their performance; personalized ads are shown only with your consent.
- Privacy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
<!-- END admob -->

<!-- IF unityads -->
### Unity Ads (Unity Technologies)
- **Data:** advertising ID, device information, ad interactions.
- Privacy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
<!-- END unityads -->

<!-- IF iap -->
### In-app purchases
Payments are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details. We receive only the purchase confirmation (product, transaction ID).
<!-- END iap -->

## 2. Consent and your choices
<!-- IF ads -->
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland users:** before ads are shown, a consent form asks for permission for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with **About → Privacy settings**.
- **iOS:** the app asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.
<!-- END ads -->
- You can turn off ad personalization from your device's ad settings.
